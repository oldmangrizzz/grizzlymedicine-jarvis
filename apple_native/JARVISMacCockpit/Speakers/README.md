# People JARVIS Recognizes

Use the Cockpit panel **People JARVIS recognizes** to add or remove family members.

## Add someone

1. Click **Add a person**.
2. Type their name and who they are to you, like “wife,” “son,” or “father.”
3. Pass the Mac to them.
4. They read the on-screen script and record their voice.
5. Play it back.
6. If it sounds right, click **Sounds good — keep it**.
7. Robert approves with Touch ID.
8. JARVIS saves the voice as `_local_voice/speakers/<uuid>.wav` and updates the voice baseline.

## Remove someone

1. Click **Remove** next to their name.
2. Type their name exactly.
3. Robert approves with Touch ID.

## Defaults

Every added person starts with permission to listen and speak with JARVIS: `listen` and `speak_with`. Only the operator can change JARVIS's identity.
